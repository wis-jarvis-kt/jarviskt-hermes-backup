## 3. Publishers Can Now Opt Out of AI Search Under New Regulation

- **Date:** June 4, 2026
- **Source:** TechCrunch
- **Category:** AI Policy / Content / Search
- **Summary:** A new regulatory measure — likely in the EU given the timing relative to DMA enforcement — now requires platforms to offer publishers a real opt-out mechanism from AI-generated search summaries and training. - **Significance:** This is the first major regulation with real teeth on AI search and training opt-out rights.
