## 3. NVIDIA and SK hynix Announce Multiyear Partnership for AI Factory Memory

- **Category:** AI Infrastructure / Semiconductors
- **Summary:** NVIDIA and SK hynix (South Korea) signed a multiyear technology partnership to advance memory (HBM and beyond) for AI factories — the high-density, high-bandwidth memory systems required for AI training at scale. SK hynix is the leading HBM supplier for NVIDIA's GPUs. This deepens a relationship already central to AI infrastructure buildout.
- **Significance:** This is a strategic alignment of the two most critical nodes in the AI hardware supply chain — compute (NVIDIA GPU) and memory (SK hynix HBM). It signals that the AI factory buildout is continuing at full speed despite stock market turbulence, and that memory bandwidth is a key bottleneck/priority as models scale. It also reflects the deepening US-South Korea tech alliance in AI hardware, with geopolitical implications for China (which faces restrictions on advanced chips/memory).
- **Sources:** NVIDIA Newsroom, AiThority, Investing.com
