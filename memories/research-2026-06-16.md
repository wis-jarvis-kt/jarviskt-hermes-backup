## 1. SpaceX to Acquire Cursor for $60B in Stock, Days After Blockbuster IPO

- **Category:** AI / M&A / SpaceX
- **Summary:** SpaceX has agreed to acquire AI coding startup Cursor in a $60 billion stock deal, just days after SpaceX's historic IPO and less than two months after announcing a tie-up between the two. The deal is meant to help SpaceX's AI division — built around Elon Musk's xAI, which SpaceX merged with earlier this year — catch up to major AI labs. SpaceX told investors its total addressable market is ~$28 trillion, with $26 trillion centered on AI efforts. Cursor was on track to raise a $2B round at $50B valuation before SpaceX intervened; the deal structure included a $10B break-up fee.
- **Significance:** Post-IPO, SpaceX stock jumped from $135 to $200+ per share, adding ~$1 trillion in market cap in days. The acquisition underscores the massive AI infrastructure bets SpaceX is making post-IPO, and raises questions about xAI's internal struggles — all 11 of Musk's xAI co-founders had left by March, and xAI faced controversies over deepfake generation. This is the biggest AI acquisition since the IPO wave began.

## 2. ChatGPT's Market Share Slips Below 50% for First Time

- **Category:** AI / Competition / Market Share
- **Summary:** OpenAI's ChatGPT has dropped below 50% global AI assistant market share for the first time, according to Sensor Tower's State of AI Report for 2026. ChatGPT still leads with ~1.1B monthly users, but Gemini now holds 27.7% and Claude 10.3%. OpenAI's DoD partnership in February triggered a measurable spike in uninstalls, suggesting brand trust matters to users. Meanwhile, AI app spending reached $4.2B in H1 2026 (vs $1.83B in H1 2025), indicating the industry is shifting from pure growth to monetization.
- **Significance:** The 50% threshold is symbolic but real — ChatGPT's dominance was assumed permanent; its erosion signals a maturing, more competitive AI assistant market. Claude's 13% paying subscriber conversion rate leads the field, a key metric for investors evaluating sustainable AI revenue models. The DoD-driven uninstall spike is a warning sign for AI companies pursuing enterprise/government contracts at the expense of consumer trust.
- **URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/chatgpts-market-share-slips-below-50-for-first-time/

## 3. Threads Reaches 500M Monthly Users, Adds Personalization and Community Features

- **Category:** Social Media / Platform Competition
- **Summary:** Meta's Threads has reached 500 million monthly active users, adding 100M users in the past 10 months. The platform launched "Your Algo" — a private feed customization tool letting users control what they see for 1–7 days without posting publicly — rolling out in the U.S., Canada, UK, Australia, and New Zealand. Threads also expanded Communities out of beta with a new Discovery Hub, and is rolling out Live Chats to all communities by July with co-hosting and quote-to-feed features.
- **Significance:** Threads is now a genuine X/Twitter competitor with real scale. The "Your Algo" feature is novel — no rival platform offers private, temporary feed control. The 500M milestone confirms Meta's ability to migrate users from Instagram into a standalone social product. Live Chats expansion closes the real-time conversation gap with X, and the quiet/loud contrast (video-centric rivals vs. Threads' text-first approach) positions it differently in the market.
- **URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/threads-adds-new-personalization-and-community-features-as-it-reaches-500m-monthly-users/
